Tahiti Pearl Beach Resort

Price: $780 Per night

Make your escape to the tranquil Tahiti Pearl Beach Resort, which offers outstanding comfort and spectacular waterfront views in the lush, tropical surroundings of the 900-metre-long Lafayette Beach. With its stunning oceanfront location on historic Matavai Bay, this destination resort offers easy access to a wealth of activities and attractions.

Hôtel Reva Tahiti

Price: $380 Per night

Welcome to Hôtel Reva Tahiti, your contemporary address in the heart of Papeete. Ideally located just steps from the waterfront, the market, and ferries to Moorea, our hotel combines modern comfort with Polynesian charm. Our air-conditioned rooms and studios offer space, quality bedding, a kitchenette (depending on the category), and some feature a terrace. Enjoy a tropical or continental breakfast each morning before setting out to explore the capital on foot.

Boutique Hotel Kon Tiki Tahiti

Price: $180 Per night

The Kon Tiki TAHITI has a history of travel just from the mention of its name. Once a boat, then a hotel, today it has a new architecture. Rooted in Ma'ohi culture, its residents will experience a total immersion. In the heart of Pape'ete of yesteryear, opposite the waterfront ferry dock, the retro charm of this singular hotel makes it a suspended parenthesis outside time.
