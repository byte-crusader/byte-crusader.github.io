Getting Around Taniti!

Getting around Taniti is simple, with several transportation options available to visitors. Whether you prefer exploring on foot, renting a vehicle, or using public transportation, there are convenient ways to experience the island.

Public Transportation

Public buses operate throughout Taniti City every day from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Private bus services are also available for visitors traveling to other parts of the island.

Bicycle Rentals

Bikes and helmets are available for visitors, and helmets are required by law. Cycling is a convenient way to explore Taniti City's relatively flat streets.

Taxis

Taxis are available throughout Taniti City and provide convenient transportation for visitors traveling between hotels, restaurants, and nearby attractions.
