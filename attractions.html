Come explore the island!

Discover everything Taniti has to offer, from beautiful tropical beaches to adventurous rainforests. Whether you enjoy relaxing by the ocean, exploring nature, or experiencing local culture, there is something for everyone.

Tropical Beaches

Relax along Taniti's beautiful sandy beaches surrounding Yellow Leaf Bay. Visitors can enjoy swimming, snorkeling, sunbathing, and exploring the island's coastline.

Rainforest Adventures

looking for something more adventurous, rainforest zip lining offers a unique way to experience the island's natural beauty.

The Active Volcano

Come visit the mountain ranges and discover the active volcano, heliptoper rides are an amzing way to experience the sites.
