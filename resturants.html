Fresh Seafood - Yellowfin

Located at Marina Taina in Puna'auia, Yellowfin specializes in fresh local seafood, including yellowfin tuna. Enjoy fresh fish and waterfront dining while experiencing the flavors of French Polynesia.

Local Tahitian Cuisine - Maru Maru

Located in Papeete, Maru Maru celebrates local ingredients and Tahitian culinary traditions. Visitors can experience Polynesian-inspired cooking and discover the unique flavors of the islands.

Pan-Asian Cuisine - San Sushi Bar

San Sushi Bar offers Japanese cuisine, including sushi and sashimi. It is a great option for visitors looking to enjoy Asian-inspired dining.
